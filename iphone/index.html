Set up Unstall for iPhone

Last updated 2026-10-28

When an orchestrator on one of your paired Macs asks a question or wants permission, Unstall for iPhone sends you a push. Answer from the notification, or open the app to see everything waiting and approve, deny, deny with a note, or pick an answer. Workers stay quiet. The app is in private beta on TestFlight: testers are invited by email. It is not on the App Store, and there is no public link.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • A Mac running Unstall 0.10 or later. Download it from unstall.dev, or use Check for Updates if you already have it.
  • Tailscale on the iPhone and on the Mac, signed in to the same tailnet. The iPhone reaches your Mac only over Tailscale.
  • herdr is optional, as it is for the Mac app.
  • The TestFlight invite email we sent you.

Install from the invite

  1. Accept the email invite.

    Open the invite on your iPhone and follow its link.

  2. Install TestFlight.

    Get Apple’s TestFlight app from the App Store if you don’t have it.

  3. Install Unstall.

    In TestFlight, accept the invite and install Unstall. TestFlight offers each new beta build; a build stops opening when it expires, so install the newest one.

Set up

Until a Mac is paired, the app’s Macs tab shows the same three steps.

  1. Tailscale on iPhone and Mac, same tailnet.

    Install Tailscale on both, sign in to the same tailnet and turn it on. The app says “Tailscale looks connected” when it sees a Tailscale address on the iPhone. That is a hint, not a check.

  2. Install Unstall on your Mac (0.10 or later).

    The app can share the unstall.dev link so you can open it on the Mac. Tick Installed when it’s done.

  3. Pair.

    On the Mac, open Settings → Connections and click Pair new iPhone… to show a QR code. On the iPhone, open the scanner from step 3 and scan it. Each code works once and expires; if it fails, make sure Tailscale is on for both and the Mac is awake, then scan a new code. Pair each Mac you want pushes from.

Allow notifications

  • Tap Allow notifications when the app asks. If you chose Not now, turn them on in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Unstall.
  • Pushes are Time Sensitive. Leave Time Sensitive Notifications on for Unstall in the same place.
  • A Focus can hold back even Time Sensitive pushes. For each Focus you use: Settings → Focus → the Focus → Apps → add Unstall. Or, in the same place, turn on Time Sensitive Notifications. Unstall can’t change or check this for you.

Troubleshooting

No push arrives
Pushes start on your Mac. While the Mac is asleep, or Unstall isn’t running on it, no push is sent. If Tailscale is off on the Mac or the iPhone, the iPhone can’t reach the Mac: a push may show only “Agent waiting”, and answers can’t be sent. Turn Tailscale on and open the app.
Pushes stopped
Open the app once. The iPhone renews its push permission from time to time, usually without being opened; if it hasn’t run for about three weeks, opening it renews it.
“Pushes paused; items still show while the app is open.”
The iPhone couldn’t get push permission from the relay. Waiting items still show and can be answered while the app is open. It retries on its own.
“This Mac can’t send pushes yet. Update Unstall on the Mac.”
That Mac runs a version older than 0.10. Update it, then open the app on the iPhone.
Revoke or re-pair
On the Mac, open Settings → Connections and click Revoke… beside the iPhone. That iPhone can no longer see or answer that Mac’s agents and gets no more pushes from it. On the iPhone: Macs → the Mac → Forget this Mac. To re-pair, pair again from step 3.

Privacy

Pushes carry no prompt content. Your Mac sends them through Unstall’s push relay at unstall.dev to Apple, and each one says only “Agent waiting”. The relay receives the iPhone’s push token and two random IDs, and stores nothing. Commands, questions, answers and names travel only between your Mac and your iPhone, over Tailscale. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
