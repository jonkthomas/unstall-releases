Unstall for Mac

Also in the Mac app

Other Macs
Add a Mac on your Tailscale network as an SSH target and its herdr agents show in your notch. It needs herdr and key-based SSH.
Many agents
Through herdr, 15+ coding agents in one list, each with its status, prompt and elapsed time. Tested with Claude Code, Codex, pi, Grok, OpenCode and Muse.
Desktop apps
Claude and Codex desktop sessions show beside your terminal agents. Click one that’s waiting: Codex opens the exact thread, Claude opens the app.
Safe approvals
Plain Allow and Deny buttons; anything unclear stays in the terminal. High-impact approvals ask you to type the target first. Nothing is answered for you.
Answer out loud
For a question with a few plain choices, say one. Risky spoken answers are read back. Voice is off until you turn it on.
Jump back
Jump, beside an agent or on its macOS notification, opens its exact terminal pane.
Screen saver
Agent status stays in view as your display locks. It shows no prompts or output.
Updates
Checks for updates automatically; you choose Install & Relaunch or Later. No account.
